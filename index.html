GoogleGenesis Club

Where curiosity meets technology.

The Google Genesis Club was founded to provide a dynamic platform for all technology enthusiasts to explore their interests beyond the classroom. We believe in hands-on learning and creating a supportive environment where ideas can flourish.

Our events cover a wide range of topics, including artificial intelligence, web development, cybersecurity, game development, and competitive programming. Whether you're a coding pro or just starting your tech journey, our community is here to support your growth.
